Kept from the logo

Left out: fur texture, wrinkle hatching, the lettering, the plants. At 240 × 240 pixels behind glass, the mask, the tongue, the blue glasses and the hoodie are what make him recognisable.

How the device colours him

  • Frames are baked once, with the hoodie in pure magenta. The board swaps any pixel that is a mix of magenta, cream and black for the live colour, so one set of frames covers every usage and meeting colour.
  • Every other colour must stay off that mix, or it gets tinted too. That's why the mask is a neutral near-black (#2F2D29) rather than a warm brown, and the fawn and pink are a little less blue than the logo's.
  • Device colour check runs the board's own recolour (ported from firmware/src/main.cpp, in RGB565) on magenta frames and compares it with drawing the colour directly. With this palette it finds 0 stray pixels, even with the hoodie fully blue, so the firmware needs no change.
